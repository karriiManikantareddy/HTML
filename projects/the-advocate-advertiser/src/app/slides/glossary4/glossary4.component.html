Targeted Email
  • Clicks: The number of times links within the email were clicked.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): The percentage of emails sent that resulted in a click.
  • Click to Open Rate (CTOR or CTVR): The percentage of opens that resulted in a click.
  • Opens: The total number of times the email was opened.
  • Open Rate: The percentage of emails opened out of the total number of emails sent.
  • Quantity/Impressions: The total number of emails sent/delivered.
Social Media (Meta and TikTok)
  • Impressions: The total number of times your content was displayed on social platforms.
  • Clicks (All): This metric includes every interaction with your ad. It counts not only clicks on links that direct users to a specific destination but also interactions like post likes, comments, shares, clicks on your Page's name, and even expanding a photo or video to full screen.
  • Link Clicks: These are clicks on links within your ad that take users to a specific destination, such as your website, a landing page, or an app store. This metric focuses on measuring the effectiveness of your ad in driving traffic to your chosen URL.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): The percentage of impressions that resulted in any click on your ad, giving you a broad view of engagement.
  • Link Click CTR: This is the percentage of impressions that resulted in a link click, offering insight into how effectively your ad drives traffic to your intended destination.
  • Conversions: The number of desired actions taken after interacting with your ads.
  • Post Engagements: Total interactions with your posts, including clicks, likes, shares, comments, and reactions.
  • Reach: The total number of unique users who saw your ads.
