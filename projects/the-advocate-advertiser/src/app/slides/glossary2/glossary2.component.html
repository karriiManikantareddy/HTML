Branded Content
  • Article Page Views / Impressions: The number of times an article or page was viewed.
  • Comments: The number of comments on your social media posts promoting your branded content article.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): The percentage of ad impressions that resulted in a click.
  • Display Ad Clicks: The number of clicks on branded content display ads. This includes display ads from the promotional campaign and those fixed to the article page.
  • Display Ad Impressions: The number of times display ads were shown as part of the branded content campaign. This includes display ads from the promotional campaign and those fixed to the article page.
  • Likes (reactions): The number of likes on social media posts promoting the branded content article.
  • Shares: The number of times social media posts promoting the branded content article were shared.
  • Social Media Engagement: Total interactions with social media posts promoting the branded content article, including clicks, likes, comments, and shares.
  • Social Post Impressions: The number of times social media posts promoting the branded content article were seen.
  • Social Post Link Clicks: The number of times links within the social media posts were clicked. These links are set up to direct users to the branded content article.
  • Total Ad Clicks: The total clicks on all branded content ads. This includes all display ads and social media posts.
  • Total Ad Impressions: The total number of times branded content ads were shown. This includes all display ads and social media posts.
  • Total Time In-View: The total duration that the branded content campaign was visible to users.
Streaming Radio
  • Audio Starts: The number of times an audio ad was started.
  • Complete Listen Audio: The number of times an audio ad was listened to in its entirety.
  • Completion Rate: The percentage of audio ads that were listened to from start to finish.
  • Impressions: The total number of times ads were served to listeners.
  • Post-click Conversions: Conversions following a click on an ad.
  • Post-view Conversions: Conversions following exposure to an ad without a direct click.
  • Total Conversions: The total number of desired actions taken after listening to ads, including all post-view and post-click conversions.
