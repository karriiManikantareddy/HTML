Social Media Video Ads
  • Video Completion Rate (VCR): The percentage of video ads watched to completion.
  • Video P100 Views: The number of times a video was viewed to 100% of its length.
  • Video P25 Views: The number of times a video was viewed to 25% of its length.
  • Video P50 Views: The number of times a video was viewed to 50% of its length.
  • Video P75 Views: The number of times a video was viewed to 75% of its length.
  • Video Plays: The number of times your video ads were played.
  • Video Views: The total number of views on your video ads.
Google Analytics
  • Active Users: Users who have engaged with your site or app actively.
  • Conversions: The number of times users completed a goal.
  • Engaged Sessions: Sessions where a user spent a significant amount of time or interacted with your site/app.
  • Event Value: The value assigned to specific events completed by users.
  • New Users: First-time users during a selected time frame.
  • Screen/Page Views: The total number of screens or pages viewed.
  • Sessions: Total number of sessions initiated by all users.
  • Total Events: The total number of interactions on your website that were tracked as events.
  • Total Revenue: The total revenue generated from transactions.
  • Total Users: The total number of users who have interacted with your site or app.
  • Transactions: The total number of completed purchases on your site or app.
  • User Engagement Duration: The total time spent by users in engaging sessions.
