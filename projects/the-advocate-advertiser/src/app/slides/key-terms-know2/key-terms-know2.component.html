Search Engine Marketing (SEM) Terms
  • Ad Auction: The process search engines use to determine the placement of ads.
  • Ad Group: Contains one or more ads that share similar targets within a campaign.
  • Google Ads: Google's online advertising platform.
  • Keyword Match Types: Defines how closely the keyword needs to match the user's search query.
  • Landing Page: The webpage where people end up after they click on your ad.
  • Negative Keywords: Keywords that prevent an ad from being triggered by a certain word or phrase.
  • Pay-Per-Click (PPC): An advertising model where advertisers pay a fee each time one of their ads is clicked.
  • Quality Score: A metric that influences ad rank and cost per click (CPC).
  • SEM: Search Engine Marketing. Efforts to ensure a brand's content appears in search engine results.
  • Conversion Tracking: A tool to track actions taken after clicking on your ads.
Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Terms
  • Alt Text: A description of an image in your site's HTML, used for accessibility and SEO.
  • Anchor Text: The visible, clickable text in a hyperlink.
  • Backlink (Inbound Link): A link from another website to your site.
  • Keyword Density: The percentage of times a keyword appears on a web page compared to the total number of words.
  • Meta Tags: Snippets of text that describe a page's content; important for SEO.
  • Off-Page SEO: Techniques used to improve the position of a website in the SERPs, not conducted directly on the website.
  • On-Page SEO: Optimizing individual web pages to rank higher and earn more relevant traffic.
  • Organic Search: Listings on search engine results pages that appear because of their relevance to the search terms.
  • PageRank: An algorithm used by Google Search to rank web pages in their search engine results.
  • SERP (Search Engine Results Page): The page displayed by a search engine in response to a user's query.
  • SEO: Search Engine Optimization. The process of optimizing a website to increase its visibility for relevant searches.
  • Technical SEO: Website and server optimizations that help search engine spiders crawl and index your site more effectively.
