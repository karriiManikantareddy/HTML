Video
(Onsite Video, Pre-Roll, Streaming TV, Premium Programmatic)
  • 25% Complete: The number of times videos were watched to 25% of their length.
  • 50% Complete: The number of times videos were watched to 50% of their length.
  • 75% Complete: The number of times videos were watched to 75% of their length.
  • Clicks: The number of times users clicked on video ads.
  • Total Conversions: The total number of times users took a desired action after interacting with a video ad, including all post-view and post-click conversions.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): The percentage of video ad impressions that resulted in a click.
  • Impressions: The number of times video ads were displayed.
  • Post-click Conversions: Conversions following a click on the video ad.
  • Post-view Conversions: Conversions following a view of the video ad without a click.
  • Total Time in View: The cumulative time users spent watching video ads.
  • Video Completes: The number of times videos were watched entirely.
  • Video Completion Rate: The percentage of video ads watched from start to finish.
  • Video Starts: The number of times a video ad was started by a user.
  • YouTube TrueView Views: The number of views for ads on YouTube where viewers chose not to skip the ad.
SEM (Search Engine Marketing)
  • Clicks: The total number of clicks on search ads.
  • Conversions: The number of times users took a desired action after clicking on your search ads.
  • CPC (Cost Per Click): The average cost of a click on your search ads.
  • CTR (Click-Through Rate): The percentage of search ad impressions that resulted in a click.
  • Impressions: The number of times ads appeared in search results.
  • Phone Calls: The number of phone calls initiated from search ads.
  • Search Impression Share: The percentage of times your ads were shown out of the total number of times they could have been shown, based on your keywords, location targeting, and budget.
