Key terms to know
General Digital Advertising Terms
  • Ad Fatigue: When an audience becomes tired of seeing the same ad repeatedly, leading to lower engagement rates and campaign effectiveness.
  • Behavioral Targeting: Targeting users based on their browsing behavior, such as the websites they've visited or the searches they've made.
  • Bounce Rate: The percentage of visitors to a website who navigate away from the site after viewing only one page.
  • Clicks: The count of times users have clicked on an ad.
  • Click-Through Rate (CTR): The percentage of impressions that led to a click. Calculated as (Clicks / Impressions) * 100.
  • Conversion Rate: The percentage of users who took a desired action after clicking on an ad. Calculated as (Conversions / Clicks) * 100.
  • Cost Per Action (CPA): The cost associated with a specific action being taken. Calculated as (Total Cost / Number of Actions).
  • Cost Per Click (CPC): The amount paid for each click on an ad.
  • Cost Per Impression (CPM): Cost per thousand impressions. Reflects the amount paid for every thousand impressions of an ad.
  • Engagement Rate: A measure of the level of engagement that users have with an ad.
  • Frequency: The average number of times an ad is shown to the same user over a specific period.
  • Geo-Targeting: The practice of delivering ads to users based on their geographic locations.
  • Impressions: The number of times an ad is displayed, regardless of whether it was clicked or not.
  • Lookalike Audience: A way to reach new people who are likely to be interested in your business because they're similar to your best existing customers.
  • Programmatic Advertising: The automated buying and selling of online advertising space.
  • Remarketing/Retargeting: Serving ads to users who have previously visited your website or mobile app.
  • Return on Ad Spend (ROAS): Calculates the return directly from advertising spend. Calculated as (Revenue from Ad Spend / Cost of Ad Spend).
  • Return on Investment (ROI): A measure of the profitability of the advertising campaign.
  • View-Through Conversions: Conversions that happen after a user sees an ad but does not click on it.
